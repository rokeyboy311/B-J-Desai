# Bina Enterprise ERP - Architecture & Implementation Audit

## 1. Module Cross-Check & Gap Resolution Summary
- **Dashboard**:
  - Live capital calculations: Total Investment = Investor Investment + Bill Investment. Supervisor cost excluded from active investment pool.
  - Live stock inventory summary: Derived directly from canonical `stock_ledger` movements (`IN` via Purchases, `OUT` via Material Supply & Site Supply).
  - Supervisor cost reconciliation: Compared against Site Supply material burn with status matching (`MATCHED`, `SURPLUS`, `DEFICIT`).
  - Labour salary & attendance metrics: Accurately reflects Day-wise and Work-wise gross earnings, upads, deductions, and outstanding balances.
- **Investor Category**:
  - Full CRUD operations implemented: Create (including Initial Amount & Date), Edit, View (detailed ledger & transaction history), and Delete.
  - Automatic balance formula: `Total Contribution - Total Withdrawal (Upad)`.
- **Investment Category (3-Part Structure)**:
  - Part 1: Investment From Investors (full CRUD: Create, Edit, View, Delete).
  - Part 2: Investment From Bills (full CRUD: Create, Edit, View, Delete).
  - Part 3: Combined Records with visible navigation tab button and aggregate totals.
- **Material Purchase & Stock**:
  - Full Purchase entry with Unit of Measurement (UOM), supplier, rate, tax, and auto-entry into `stock_ledger` as `IN`.
  - Reversal of stock on edit or delete of purchase transactions.
- **Material Supply (External GST Bill)**:
  - Comprehensive GST supply logging (Quantity, Rate, GST %, Transport, Discount, Payment Status, Payment Method, Ref).
  - Recorded as `OUT` in `stock_ledger` with automatic stock reversal on update/delete.
- **Site Supply (Internal Site Issue)**:
  - Multi-item material issue per record linked to Location and Labour Team.
  - Direct deduction from inventory stock as `OUT` with full transactional reversal on update/delete.
- **Labour Attendance**:
  - Completely purged legacy "TC" terminology; replaced with standard "Day-wise" and "Work-wise" models.
  - Work-wise attendance captures work description, quantity, unit (Nos, Brass, Sq.Ft, etc.), rate per unit, team/labour selection, and auto-calculated total.
  - Attendance records view with edit and delete capabilities.
- **Labour Records & Balances**:
  - Individual labour summaries aggregating attendance, gross wages, upad advances, direct payments, and net balances.
  - Support for direct salary disbursement and upad entries.
- **Supervisor Cost**:
  - Clean expense tracking categorized by supervisor name, date, amount, and payment details.
